## 3. Engineering operations, systems integration and acceptance

**Core practices**

1. **Discover the operating problem:** model user roles, data movement, business rules and failure paths.
2. **Design boundaries:** choose fit-for-purpose runtimes, database scopes, API contracts, authorization and human approval checkpoints.
3. **Build and verify:** TypeScript/React/Node/Deno, SQL, automated tests, manual acceptance and real-device observations where applicable.
4. **Release with evidence:** change review, environment checks, deployment health, rollback considerations and post-release verification.
5. **Close the loop:** distinguish confirmed behavior from assumptions, then iterate from the observed results.

**Enterprise experience:** 15+ years spanning business systems analysis, enterprise delivery, POS, ERP, WMS, logistics, data integration, QA/UAT and release coordination. This experience informs the engineering approach, especially where reliability and operational traceability matter.

## 4. Security, privacy and accountable AI

- Privacy-sensitive product data belongs in controlled environments and access-scoped services, not public profiles or demo fixtures.
